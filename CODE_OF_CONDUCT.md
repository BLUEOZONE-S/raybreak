# Code of conduct

This is a small hobby research project. Be respectful, patient and constructive.

- Welcome newcomers and answer beginner questions kindly.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Assume good intent; ask for clarification before assuming otherwise.
- Keep discussion on topic and do not share anyone's private information.
- Respect other people's intellectual property: do not post material you are not allowed to share.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block people who repeatedly break these rules. Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting the repository owner through GitHub.
